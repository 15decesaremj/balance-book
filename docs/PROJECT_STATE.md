# Project state

Updated: 2026-07-19

## Current milestone

Balance Book is on the 1.1.1 V1 feature-release candidate line. The application is a local-first Windows x64 financial operations desktop app with editable daily forecasting, per-card spending power, revolving and installment debt tracking, account-level funding guidance, portable encrypted backup/restore, and repeatable Windows packaging.

No signed public binary is claimed. The current 1.1.1 feature tree passed 547 synthetic unit and integration tests across 49 files, formatting, lint, strict type-checking, the 183-file privacy gate, production packaging, serious/critical accessibility checks, responsive screenshot review, and the complete isolated Electron journey. This is strong local owner-candidate evidence, not public-production readiness: signing, clean-history remote verification, fresh-disposable-profile artifact lifecycle, and the remaining publication gates in [PUBLIC_RELEASE_READINESS.md](PUBLIC_RELEASE_READINESS.md) remain open.

## Implemented product foundation

- A deterministic financial engine uses exact integer cents and timezone-free dates for recurrence, expected/protected forecasts, account and consolidated low points, card-cycle settlement, revolving debt, installment amortization, transfer timing, loans, receivables, rewards, net worth, reconciliation, and scenarios.
- The versioned SQLite data layer provides foreign keys, transactions, WAL, migration snapshots, audit events, import lineage, onboarding drafts, and profile-scoped reads and writes.
- Overview answers per-card Spending Power from one reconciled total-position runway, shows independently dated current-cycle daily-closing bank lows plus money owed, and preserves intraday cash diagnostics separately. A card's account-risk window ends on the later of its limiting total-position date or payment date, so an unrelated later account episode stays in Account coverage. Funding Actions describe the first and deepest closes in each account's next contiguous below-floor episode, retain later episodes as separate planning decisions, and reserve projected receivable coverage sequentially so it cannot be promised twice.
- Guided pages edit cards and statement history, cash accounts and guardrails, recurring income, routed paychecks with account-specific early arrival and effective-dated routing phases under one paycheck stream, projected raises/bonuses, bills, transfers, loans, committed refinances, receivables, assets, scenarios, and reconciliation.
- The Refinance planner supports one or multiple payoff loans, date-specific payoff projection, separate closing/payoff/first-payment dates, financed versus cash-paid fees, principal cash contribution, excess proceeds to a selected account, explicit commitment, immutable offer snapshots, automatic effective-dated secured-asset relinking, durable history, pre-close reverse cancellation, and chronological refinancing of a prior replacement loan.
- Committed refinance plans flow through the native daily ledger and effective liability set: only real closing cash and proceeds touch bank accounts, old payments stop on payoff, replacement payments begin on their selected date, secured assets follow a continuous stacked collateral chain, and cash lows, Spending Power inputs, and net worth update from the same records.
- Financial Records provides a canonical advanced library, provenance, forecast impact, related records, and direct editing without maintaining a separate calculation model.
- Charts provides a selectable two-year view centered on the current financial date: one year of available observed/reported history and one year of native expected projections for total position, cash accounts, cards, loans, investments, Money Owed, and net worth. Missing history remains blank, observed and modeled evidence stay distinguishable, and summary cards report ranges, monthly averages, carry, and trajectories without creating a second ledger.
- Every substantive loading surface now uses a theme-aware rounded shimmer skeleton with a reduced-motion fallback and accessible live status. Cash-changing and destructive forms use immediate reentrancy locks, setup exit flushes the latest draft, stale scenarios cannot be saved, long-list editors reveal and focus their form, and backup/restore/import/export/reset actions cannot overlap.
- Expected and protected forecasts roll from the current financial date. Transfers retain consolidated ownership while in transit, and recommendations reserve source capacity jointly.
- Cards preserve coming-due statements, open-cycle spending, future baseline assumptions, older history, payment policy, reported total/carrying snapshots, and explicit incomplete-timing states as distinct records. Multiple future payments can be scheduled on independent dates while autopay supplies only the remaining policy amount. Recording an actual payment preserves paid-in-full history, carries only a real underpayment, and applies overpayment as later balance credit rather than negative carry. Cards, charge cards, and lines of credit report statement due, current balance, available credit, utilization, and balance carrying independently.
- Revolving accounts support effective-dated retirement/reactivation. Closure blocks purchases, recurring activity, new cycle openings, baselines, Spending Power, and advisor use from the selected date while retaining final earlier cycles, debt, later payoff cash, and history.
- Money Owed supports explicit-release recurring receivables: future installments accrue on their own dates without entering today's owed total or a bank account, and full or partial releases reduce only the selected occurrence while depositing into the account chosen for that release. Funding guidance can show this owed value as potential coverage without treating it as automatic cash.
- Installment loans accept complete or partial lender facts, label calculated values, and support monthly/biweekly fully amortizing and balloon/bullet structures. Dated schedules separate total bank draft from debt service, expose interest/principal/remaining balance, distinguish contractual maturity from modeled payoff, and carry regular-draft overrides and additional principal through cash, liability, refinance, and net-worth outputs.
- Portable backup V2 encrypts a profile's managed financial state, settings, audit, debt metadata, refinance plans, immutable offer snapshots, payoff/collateral lineage, receivable timing anchors, and import lineage while leaving destination identity and sign-in password local. Restore validates the envelope and record graph, creates an encrypted safety copy, rejects ownership conflicts, and replaces data transactionally. Migrations through schema 21 repair validated legacy refinance lineage; schemas 22 through 26 add debt metadata, loan-payment treatment, revolving-account lifecycle, installment amortization structure, and optional bill-relative receivable timing with legacy-safe defaults. Schema 27 adds durable occurrence identity and target metadata so historical partial receipts remain stable through later amount, destination, recurrence-day, bill-anchor, cancellation, and restore changes. Schema 28 narrowly repairs a proven legacy paycheck shape by converting its early-account transfer into a real early-arriving income allocation while retaining the cancelled transfer pair and an audit record. Schema 29 moves only untouched imported static receivables with a proven no-date placeholder onto an editable first-of-next-month expected receipt, preserving unconfirmed-date treatment, lineage, audit history, and all edited or previously settled rows.
- JSON/CSV export is explicitly plaintext, collision-free, formula-escaped, and separate from portable recovery.
- The Electron shell defaults to dark mode and uses context isolation, renderer sandboxing, a narrow typed bridge, validated IPC, restrictive navigation/permission behavior, custom local protocol, CSP, hardened fuses, and single-instance application identity.
- Automated engine, domain, database, contract, migration, backup, renderer, accessibility, and packaged-application checks are part of the `pnpm verify` baseline. The current tree's 547-test suite covers closing-based funding episodes, sequential receivable coverage, release-only recurring accruals and partial releases, dated card-payment/autopay/carry and credit behavior, Charts debt/net-worth invariance, responsive layouts, refinance persistence, backups, privacy, and dependent page behavior; formatting, lint, strict type-checking, production packaging, and the complete Playwright Electron journey passed with it.

## Known limits

- The local sign-in password is an application privacy boundary; the live database is not encrypted at rest.
- V1 has no telemetry, bank sync, cloud service, remote access, auto-update, password recovery, or automatic money movement.
- Plaintext exports require the user to provide storage protection.
- A backup cannot be recovered without its backup password, and two independently active profiles are not merged automatically.
- Windows 11 x64 is the supported V1 desktop target; other operating systems and architectures are not claimed.
- No formal security, accessibility, financial-advice, or third-party legal certification has been completed.
