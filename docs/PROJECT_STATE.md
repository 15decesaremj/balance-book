# Project state

Updated: 2026-07-23

## Current milestone

Balance Book is on the 2.0.7 feature-release candidate line. The application is a local-first Windows x64 financial control center organized around current truth, actionable financial check-in, and safe decisions while retaining editable daily forecasting, per-card spending power, revolving and installment debt tracking, account-level funding guidance, portable encrypted backup/restore, and repeatable Windows packaging.

No signed public binary is claimed. Version 2.0.7 is the installed verified unsigned owner candidate. A trusted Windows Authenticode certificate and fresh-disposable-profile lifecycle remain mandatory before a public beta installer or automatic-update feed may be published.

## Implemented product foundation

- A deterministic financial engine uses exact integer cents and timezone-free dates for recurrence, expected/protected forecasts, account and consolidated low points, card-cycle settlement, revolving debt, installment amortization, transfer timing, loans, receivables, rewards, net worth, reconciliation, and scenarios.
- The versioned SQLite data layer provides foreign keys, transactions, WAL, migration snapshots, audit events, import lineage, onboarding drafts, and profile-scoped reads and writes.
- First-run setup asks six plain-language applicability questions and dynamically skips income, bill, or card starter steps that do not apply. The same answers hide unused income, bills, cards, loans, money-owed, and asset surfaces without deleting records or changing forecast mechanics. Searchable Settings opens the exact local control and can re-enable every section.
- Profile preferences add only backward-compatible defaults, so existing profiles retain every visible surface. A database created by a newer application schema is refused before any pragma, migration, or backup write, and the failed connection is closed cleanly.
- The header uses a number-only attention control and one responsive financial center with keyboard-accessible Notices, Bills, and Balances views. Notices derive stable, deduplicated actions and updates from canonical state; exact account warnings lead with the first and deepest cash shortfalls and keep possible Money Owed coverage secondary. Bills reads expected outflows from the complete daily ledger while excluding income, internal transfers, completed payments, and hypothetical activity. Balances reuses the authoritative calculated account/card read models rather than maintaining a second ledger. Profile-scoped schema 34 stores only condition fingerprints and presentation timestamps, remains recovery-safe, and is intentionally excluded from portable financial backup.
- The unread notification count is the header control itself, matches the Log out control's height, becomes a same-position circular close control while open, and turns to a quiet zero after **Mark all read** without deleting financial conditions. Bills and Balances omit redundant count pills. The in-app brand mark uses the exact canonical centered artwork used by the Windows application icon family.
- The document-level financial-center portal carries its own exact Fluent theme boundary, preserving the intended spacing, typography, glass treatment, and rounded cards in dark and light modes without returning to transformed-ancestor positioning. A one-time presentation-contract advance re-alerts conditions acknowledged under the broken 2.0.3 presentation; subsequent **Mark all read** state persists normally.
- The financial-center surface now holds one fixed geometry across Notices, Bills, and Balances, with a sliding segmented selection, directional reduced-motion-aware content transitions, contained unread dots, and wrapping account labels. Phone and desktop geometry are asserted from computed bounds rather than screenshots alone.
- Explicit renderer read models distinguish the last user-reported account or issuer balance and date, net canonical activity after that source, the calculated position through the current financial date, and future projection. Overview never relabels an older source balance as reported today merely because the native engine rolled it forward.
- Overview answers per-card Spending Power from one reconciled total-position runway, shows independently dated current-cycle daily-closing bank lows plus money owed, and preserves intraday cash diagnostics separately. A card's account-risk window ends on the later of its limiting total-position date or payment date, so an unrelated later account episode stays in Account coverage. Funding Actions describe the first and deepest closes in each account's next contiguous below-floor episode, retain later episodes as separate planning decisions, and reserve projected receivable coverage sequentially so it cannot be promised twice.
- Overview now shows each card's earliest unresolved contractual due date separately from modeled cash-payment timing. Reset badges distinguish future, today, stale, and unavailable close dates. Each card separates current posted debt and the latest statement balance, then adds Still owed only when actual carrying-balance evidence exists. Projected lows place open money owed at the exact limiting total-position date beside the total and account lows. Generic setup/runway tags no longer compete with those facts. A persistent per-account display preference can hide a cash-account tile without removing that account from forecasts, card runway, protected minimums, or funding warnings.
- Overview defaults to sorting cards by the shortest remaining current statement period and offers reverse-period, name, available-spend, current-balance, and last-statement orders. Missing timing or balance evidence remains last instead of masquerading as zero. Each card also shows the lowest total cash plus open receivables on or after the first contractual due date after its next due date, using the native expected or protected daily forecast rather than an exact-date snapshot.
- Every visible Overview checking account and card is now one keyboard-accessible detail surface. Now preserves the compact Balance/Transaction workflow and defaults to the current financial date; Activity shows source snapshots, recent canonical records, corrections, reversals, statements, and relevant audit envelopes; Plan exposes scheduled actions and links to the exact existing editors. Cash deposits or withdrawals, card purchases or credits, issuer current-balance snapshots, and latest-statement corrections preview their direct effect, return focus to the opener, preserve failures in place, and link to complete settings.
- Overview quick updates write only canonical audited account, card, cycle, or cash-event records. Balance saves preserve account floors and behavior; card current-total saves do not rewrite statements; statement corrections preserve terms and payment evidence and warn only when the edit is unusual after due date. Same-day transactions are explicitly after the balance snapshot, refresh the native forecast once, and propagate through account lows, funding guidance, purchase advice, revolving debt, net worth, and card Spending Power without a parallel ledger.
- Overview can also record one canonical expense from the card-runway header. Cash expenses reduce the selected account immediately; card expenses become additional current-cycle activity and leave cash unchanged until settlement. Optional reimbursable and shared treatments create linked Money Owed records for exactly 100% or 50% of the expense, including deterministic odd-cent handling, through one audited SQLite transaction that rolls back completely on failure.
- The authenticated shell now has exactly five stable primary destinations: Overview, Forecast, Accounts, Planning, and Settings. Existing advanced routes remain available through concise section navigation; Accounts and Planning have focused hubs, Cards progressively reveals one card's complete management workspace, Settings uses five task-oriented categories, and Refinance guides the full existing model through three steps with a persistent offer summary.
- Guided pages edit cards and statement history, cash accounts and safety settings, recurring income, routed paychecks with account-specific early arrival and effective-dated routing phases under one paycheck stream, projected raises/bonuses, bills, transfers, loans, committed refinances, money owed, assets, scenarios, and balance checks.
- Accounts includes a dedicated **Bills & subscriptions** workspace over the existing recurring cash-event records. Each bill can change amount, timing, cadence, end date, payment source, and status; card bills explicitly choose whether they are already included in the entered cycle total or add new card activity. Reimbursable and shared bills create linked, occurrence-dated Money Owed without creating bank cash before receipt.
- The Refinance planner supports one or multiple payoff loans, date-specific payoff projection, separate closing/payoff/first-payment dates, financed versus cash-paid fees, principal cash contribution, excess proceeds to a selected account, explicit commitment, immutable offer snapshots, automatic effective-dated secured-asset relinking, durable history, pre-close reverse cancellation, and chronological refinancing of a prior replacement loan.
- Committed refinance plans flow through the native daily ledger and effective liability set: only real closing cash and proceeds touch bank accounts, old payments stop on payoff, replacement payments begin on their selected date, secured assets follow a continuous stacked collateral chain, and cash lows, Spending Power inputs, and net worth update from the same records.
- Activity & records presents a searchable, filterable timeline over the existing canonical balances, cash/card activity, statements, payments, income, bills, receivables, loans, assets, reconciliations, imports, reversals, and audit envelopes. It retains the canonical advanced library, provenance, forecast impact, related records, and direct editing without maintaining a separate calculation model; forecast event labels trace to their producing record.
- Charts provides a selectable two-year view centered on the current financial date: one year of available observed/reported history and one year of native expected projections for total position, cash accounts, cards, loans, investments, Money Owed, and net worth. Cash Forecast also exposes net worth as its fifth primary series after the two checking accounts. Investment projections can use an expected annual return, fixed monthly contributions, and gross-pay-based employee and employer contributions without reducing take-home cash a second time. Missing history remains blank, observed and modeled evidence stay distinguishable, and summary cards report ranges, monthly averages, carry, and trajectories without creating a second ledger.
- Cash Forecast leads with conservative net monthly free cash flow: the weakest rolling three-month expected change in cash plus Money Owed within a clean future year after current and near-term timing noise clears. Known scheduled card payments in that run remain included and their average effect is disclosed separately; extra-paycheck months and later payoff improvements cannot inflate the current base budget.
- Credit Cards estimates monthly interest only on positive carried balances, supports ordinary and whole-balance promotional APRs, and reports missing applicable APRs explicitly. Experimental forecast accrual is off by default and requires both the profile-wide and per-card controls; locked statements stay authoritative.
- Every substantive loading surface now uses a theme-aware rounded shimmer skeleton with a reduced-motion fallback and accessible live status. Cash-changing and destructive forms use immediate reentrancy locks, setup exit flushes the latest draft, purchase evaluation and saved-scenario actions share one immediate lock, stale scenarios cannot be saved, long-list editors reveal and focus their form on first and repeat activation, and backup/restore/import/export/reset actions cannot overlap.
- A route-wide control audit now binds each editable surface to persisted-response repopulation and downstream recalculation. Cards, cycles, scheduled payments, recorded payments, loans, income, cash events, receivables, assets, reconciliation, settings, setup decisions, data imports, and scenarios expose local progress and failure states instead of silently closing or leaving stale values. Parent loan and statement-cycle deletion is blocked while linked payment history remains.
- Expected and protected forecasts roll from the current financial date. Transfers retain consolidated ownership while in transit, and recommendations reserve source capacity jointly.
- Cards preserve coming-due statements, open-cycle spending, future baseline assumptions, older history, payment policy, reported total/carrying snapshots, and explicit incomplete-timing states as distinct records. Multiple future payments can be scheduled on independent dates while autopay supplies only the remaining policy amount. A scheduled forecast payment does not reduce actual debt until it is confirmed or paid. Recording an actual payment stores the cash account used, preserves paid-in-full history, carries only a real underpayment, applies overpayment as later balance credit rather than negative carry, and emits the cash effect exactly once. Carry and delinquency remain separate: a recorded partial or special-financing payment can retain financed statement carry without being labeled overdue, while an unresolved statement after its due date remains past due. Cards, charge cards, and lines of credit report statement due, current balance, available credit, utilization, and balance carrying independently.
- Revolving accounts support effective-dated retirement/reactivation. Closure blocks purchases, recurring activity, new cycle openings, baselines, Spending Power, and advisor use from the selected date while retaining final earlier cycles, debt, later payoff cash, and history.
- Money Owed supports explicit-release recurring receivables: future installments accrue on their own dates without entering today's owed total or a bank account, and Forecast names each accrual by source and purpose. Full or partial releases reduce only the selected occurrence while depositing into the account chosen for that release. An optional automatic receipt applies one deposit oldest-first across open balances and recurring occurrences while retaining occurrence-specific audit lineage and rejecting over-receipts atomically. Funding guidance can show this owed value as potential coverage without treating it as automatic cash.
- Installment loans accept complete or partial lender facts, label calculated values, and support monthly/biweekly fully amortizing and balloon/bullet structures. Dated schedules separate total bank draft from debt service, expose interest/principal/remaining balance, distinguish contractual maturity from modeled payoff, and carry regular-draft overrides and additional principal through cash, liability, refinance, and net-worth outputs. Funding-account and schedule edits reconcile future linked drafts transactionally, preserve historical occurrences, and reject an incompatible change without partially mutating either the loan or cash ledger.
- Portable backup V3 encrypts a profile's managed financial state, experience preferences, settings, audit, debt metadata, refinance plans, immutable offer snapshots, payoff/collateral lineage, receivable timing anchors, and import lineage while leaving destination identity and sign-in password local. Strict V2 backups normalize to V3 defaults before restore. Restore validates the envelope and record graph, creates an encrypted safety copy, rejects ownership conflicts, and replaces data transactionally. Migrations through schema 21 repair validated legacy refinance lineage; schemas 22 through 26 add debt metadata, loan-payment treatment, revolving-account lifecycle, installment amortization structure, and optional bill-relative receivable timing with legacy-safe defaults. Schema 27 adds durable occurrence identity and target metadata so historical partial receipts remain stable through later amount, destination, recurrence-day, bill-anchor, cancellation, and restore changes. Schema 28 narrowly repairs a proven legacy paycheck shape by converting its early-account transfer into a real early-arriving income allocation while retaining the cancelled transfer pair and an audit record. Schema 29 moves only untouched imported static receivables with a proven no-date placeholder onto an editable first-of-next-month expected receipt, preserving unconfirmed-date treatment, lineage, audit history, and all edited or previously settled rows. Recovery-safe schema 30 adds the per-account Overview visibility preference; schema 31 adds profile-scoped default view, spacing, motion, and Overview-section preferences; schema 32 adds the account actually used for a recorded statement payment with legacy-safe fallback to the card funding account; schema 33 adds an explicit legacy-safe flag for transactions that occur after a same-day reported balance snapshot; schema 34 adds device-local notification presentation metadata without duplicating financial truth; recovery-safe schema 35 adds optional investment return and gross-pay contribution assumptions without changing existing assets; recovery-safe schema 36 adds per-card interest-forecast and whole-balance promotional-rate controls with legacy-safe defaults that preserve existing forecasts.
- JSON/CSV export is explicitly plaintext, collision-free, formula-escaped, and separate from portable recovery.
- The Electron shell defaults to dark mode and uses context isolation, renderer sandboxing, a narrow typed bridge, validated IPC, restrictive navigation/permission behavior, custom local protocol, CSP, hardened fuses, and single-instance application identity. Theme and experience controls live together in searchable Settings; desktop navigation can collapse to its accessible icon rail and remembers that choice. ## Known limits

- The local sign-in password is an application privacy boundary; the live database is not encrypted at rest.
- Balance Book has no telemetry, bank sync, cloud service, remote access, password recovery, or automatic money movement. Signed public builds may contact only the fixed GitHub-hosted software-update feed and immutable package; unsigned owner-test builds keep that path disabled.
- Plaintext exports require the user to provide storage protection.
- A backup cannot be recovered without its backup password, and two independently active profiles are not merged automatically.
- Windows 11 x64 is the supported V2 desktop target; other operating systems and architectures are not claimed.
- No formal security, accessibility, financial-advice, or third-party legal certification has been completed.
