# Product specification

Balance Book is a local-first Windows application for answering dated financial-operating questions: what cash is committed, when it settles, which account must fund it, how low cash falls, which card best fits a purchase, and what remains safe to spend after protected floors.

## V1 product rules

- Financial state is manual-first and editable. There is no bank connection, telemetry, advertising, cloud backend, or money movement.
- Cash timing and funding accounts are first-class. A positive consolidated total must not hide an underfunded account.
- Every included cash account can set its own hard minimum, preferred buffer, and transfer timing. The effective global minimum automatically includes the sum of account minimums while allowing a larger consolidated override.
- Credit-card purchases affect the applicable statement cycle and future payment date; they do not reduce cash on purchase date.
- Income is modeled as net cash reaching explicit destination accounts. A split paycheck starts from one official payday and total take-home, then applies account-specific amounts and calendar-day early-arrival timing. Raises, bonuses, and certainty changes must alter only the forecast views, accounts, and dates they actually affect.
- Receivables reduce personal economic burden but remain separate from income. Uncertain receivables cannot fund conservative or expected cash.
- Investments and tangible assets can contribute to net worth but are not deployable cash by default.
- Scenarios show before/after troughs, floor margins, account shortfalls, transfer needs, and dependencies. Multiple scenarios are evaluated together and extend through their final cash-settlement date.
- A refinance preview and its committed result use the same effective-dated mechanics. The user explicitly chooses the payoff loans, closing/payoff/first-payment dates, fee treatment, and replacement payment account, plus a closing-cash source or excess-proceeds destination when those amounts are nonzero, before the plan can alter any forecast or liability.
- A local login is an application privacy boundary, not protection from an operating-system administrator.

## Implemented in V1

- Exact integer-cent money values and timezone-free financial dates.
- Weekly, biweekly, monthly, and semimonthly recurrence expansion with constrained month ends.
- Rolling-current deterministic protected and expected forecasts with per-account and consolidated intraday troughs. Older balance snapshots roll forward through the native expected ledger before future uncertainty treatment begins.
- Outflow-before-inflow same-day ordering, manual ordering, per-account hard/preferred floors, derived global guardrails, timing-aware jointly allocated transfer suggestions, uncertainty lineage, and nonnegative consumer-facing availability paired with the raw signed margin.
- Card-cycle assignment, actual-reset and baseline-guardrail estimates, locked statement replacement, and payment-policy calculation.
- Generated future card cycles from editable timing terms, with stored statement history taking precedence and detailed card activity entering its owning cycle once.
- Revolving debt keeps the latest statement, amount currently due, current-cycle actual and projected activity, total current balance, available credit, and balance carrying as separate values. A paid-in-full account can therefore show statement history and current-cycle spending while carrying zero; issuer-reported dated snapshots remain editable evidence rather than replacing statement history.
- Cards, charge cards, and lines of credit have an effective lifecycle. The selected closure date is the first day new purchases, recurring card activity, baseline projections, generated cycle openings, Spending Power, and purchase-advisor use are blocked. Earlier cycles, existing debt, later payoff cash, and history remain visible, and reactivation clears the closure date.
- Per-card Spending Power based on the lowest bank-cash-plus-money-owed position from the owning cycle's modeled payment date forward, less the global protected threshold. Each determinate card shows the limiting-date reconciliation and a separate payment-date funding snapshot; it is never presented as available credit, and negative cash/account warnings remain visible.
- An all-card purchase advisor that ranks the same amount/date across cards using each native cycle and payment policy, reports the exact incremental scheduled payment and later cash low, includes reward context, and surfaces a prefilled timed transfer when safely fundable.
- First-class typed income and raise planning: editable cadence, destination, certainty, end date, and notes; atomic multi-account paycheck routing with fixed allocations, exactly one calculated remainder, per-account early-arrival timing, persisted destination editor order, and a three-payday preview; linked raises based on whole-paycheck take-home with an explicit destination; an optional bonus with its own explicit destination; and exact expected/protected before-and-after impact.
- Net take-home is authoritative at the cash layer. Payroll deductions already reflected in the entered deposits are not deducted again.
- Flexible installment-loan setup from complete or partial lender facts. The solver derives only mathematically constrained current balance, APR, payment, origin, term, maturity, and payoff values, labels calculated fields and assumptions, supports monthly and biweekly timing plus fully amortizing and balloon/bullet structures, and rejects contradictory or still-underdetermined combinations.
- Exact dated simple-interest amortization with interest-first payment allocation, separate total cash draft and debt-service amounts, contractual maturity distinct from modeled payoff, and regular-draft overrides distinct from additional principal. Extra principal reduces the liability on its exact date, is capped at remaining principal, changes future interest and payoff, and does not suppress regular payments.
- A native committed-refinance planner can consolidate multiple loans; derive date-specific payoffs; distinguish closing, payoff, and first-payment dates; split financed from cash-paid fees; route principal cash and excess proceeds through selected bank accounts; and compare the resulting account and consolidated lows.
- Effective-dated refinance commitments that retire each source loan and its payments on the payoff date, create the replacement liability on closing, begin its payments on the selected first-payment date, preserve unrelated loans, and carry the resulting cash and liability changes into forecasts, Spending Power inputs, and net worth.
- Refinance history and chronological stacking, including durable replacement-loan lineage, duplicate-retirement and cycle prevention, dependency-aware cancellation, audit records, and portable backup/export coverage.
- Assets secured by a payoff loan remain linked to that source before closing, relink to the replacement on closing, and advance through later refinances. The immutable offer snapshot and complete relink chain are retained so pre-close cancellation and portable restore remain reversible and auditable.
- Loans presents effective modeled values as of the app's financial date: a future replacement is zero before closing, both source and replacement may be active during a closing-to-payoff overlap, and a source is zero after payoff.
- Dated receivable settlement to one explicit deposit account, with mutually exclusive one-time, fixed-recurring, or recurring-bill-relative timing; recurring owed-balance accrual, shared-expense economics, and contractual/economic net worth remain distinct.
- Prevention of traceably duplicated direct-cash/card assumptions and net-pay/payroll-deduction assumptions.
- Two local profile shells, first-use password creation, login/logout, local persistence, and strict repository isolation.
- A progressive desktop setup slice requiring one dated cash balance and a protected floor, followed by a 15-section Assets, Liabilities, Regular Timing, and Review flow with persisted ready/reviewed/not-applicable states, warnings, and final check answers. Setup never invents transactions, statement balances, or card history.
- System, light, and dark theme preferences stored per profile, plus an accessible cash-path table paired with the chart.
- A seven-step first-forecast starter with database-backed partial-draft autosave/resume and step-specific validation, followed by the complete non-destructive sectioned setup/review flow for new and established profiles.
- Dedicated overview/forecast, baseline, cards, loans, refinance planner, receivables, assets/net worth, scenarios, reconciliation, and settings sections. Financial source records are editable through guided contextual forms, while the canonical Financial Records library provides cross-type search, lineage, audit context, and advanced complete-field editing. A grouped paycheck remains one user-facing plan: Financial Records identifies its destination legs and routes edits to Income and raises so one leg cannot silently diverge from the rest.
- Multiple accounts, paired and recurring delayed transfers with consolidated in-transit ownership, card cycles and payment terms, modeled loan payoff, partial receivable settlements, rewards, investments/tangible assets, and contractual/economic net worth.
- A daily forecast ledger that separately exposes total position, liquid cash, money owed, each account balance, and the events applied on every date, with actual/locked/estimated/hypothetical/planned state and expected-versus-conservative inclusion.
- Editable per-account minimum/preferred/lead-time/funding controls plus consolidated overrides, conservative-receipt policy, and forecast horizon in Settings; changing them recalculates Overview, Spending Power, account lows, and funding guidance without rewriting source records.
- Saved, duplicated, accepted, archived, deleted, individual, and combined scenarios, with transactional conversion into real dated commitments. Card-funded scenarios retain the card and purchase date, calculate the owning cycle's true payment date and incremental cash obligation, and preserve those semantics through conversion.
- Workbook import preview semantics, checksum protection, idempotent local seed, field lineage/confidence/warnings, protection against overwriting user-edited destinations, and deterministic reference-ledger checks. A workbook is never a runtime engine or data dependency after onboarding; it is optional read-only import evidence.
- Reconciliation history and event resolution without silently rewriting the original forecast.
- AES-256-GCM portable backups with scrypt-derived keys, validated restore, user-specific JSON/CSV export including committed refinance plans, immutable replacement-offer snapshots, payoff lineage, and collateral-relink lineage, strict graph validation, validated JSON import, migration backups, and guarded active-profile reset.

## Deliberate V1 limits

There is no bank sync, cloud backend, automatic money movement, code signing, auto-update, telemetry, or live-database encryption. Advanced lender-specific amortization, reward-category caps, and many-to-many shared-expense settlement refinements remain later work; the stored V1 records are editable and exportable.
