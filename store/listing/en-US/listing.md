# Balance Book

## Short description

Private, local cash-flow forecasting, card runway, debt tracking, and financial planning.

## Full description

Balance Book turns the financial details you already know into clear day-by-day answers:

- How much can I safely spend on each card?
- When will each cash account reach its next low?
- Will an account need funding before the next deposit?
- What will a purchase, raise, bonus, refinance, or debt payment change?

Build an editable forecast from dated cash balances, income, bills, statement cycles, scheduled
payments, receivables, loans, and assets. Review card spending power by payment date, inspect daily
cash events by account, model installment-loan payoff, and keep a complete local audit trail of the
changes you make.

Balance Book is deliberately local-first. It has no bank connection, Balance Book cloud account,
advertising, application telemetry, or money-movement feature. Your profile and financial records
stay on this Windows device unless you choose to create an export or encrypted portable backup.

## Feature summary

- Per-card current-cycle spending power and following-statement outlook
- Daily expected and conservative cash forecasts by account
- Funding warnings measured against account-specific protected minimums
- Editable income, bills, card statements and payments, receivables, loans, and assets
- Loan amortization, payoff planning, and refinance scenarios
- Net-worth and long-run cash-flow projections
- Password-protected local profiles
- AES-256-GCM encrypted portable backup and restore
- Dark mode, compact navigation, searchable settings, and responsive layouts

## Onboarding note

Choose a local profile, create a password, confirm the local-data disclosure, and enter one dated
cash balance and protected minimum. Optional Yes/No questions hide features that do not apply. A
user can reach the first forecast in about two minutes and add detailed records later.
